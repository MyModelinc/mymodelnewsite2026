Apollo for Consumer: Outbound to Verified Audiences

Apollo built a B2B outbound stack on scraped contacts and inferred intent. The consumer market never had an equivalent, for legal, structural, and signal-quality reasons. mymodel is the Apollo for consumer: a search-segment-deploy motion pointed at verified, consented behavioral audiences where every recipient is paid to engage.

Why "Apollo for consumer" hasn't existed

The Apollo motion works in B2B because the unit of action is reasonable: a salesperson reaching out to another professional in a work context. Privacy regimes accept that. Platforms tolerate it. The contact data is messy but obtainable.

That whole motion breaks the moment you point it at consumers. CCPA, CPRA, GDPR, and TCPA make scraped consumer outbound legally exposed. Email and SMS platforms enforce against it. Conversion signal collapses because the recipient never opted in. The result: every consumer-facing brand wants the speed and scale of B2B outbound and there has been no compliant, performant way to run it.

The only architecture that works on the consumer side

The replacement is not a faster scraper. It's an inverted economic model: outbound only happens when the recipient has chosen to receive paid offers, and outbound only converts when the recipient is paid for the engagement. Consent is the entry condition. Payment is the activation.

mymodel runs that motion at scale. Real consumers build a 360° behavioral Stats Card they control. Brands search the audience the same way an Apollo user filters a B2B database, by behavior, category interest, household composition, lifecycle stage, intent, and deploy Audience Orders into matched segments. Every recipient gets paid for the consent. Every send is a logged transaction.

What the brand-side workflow looks like

  • Define the audience. Filter the consumer database by category interest, behavior, household and lifecycle attributes. Save as a segment.
  • Build the Audience Order. Promotion, offer, survey, recruit, product trial. Set the budget per accepted user.
  • Deploy. The order is delivered into the segment. Users decide whether to accept. Acceptance is the moment of consent and the moment they earn.
  • Measure. Acceptance rate, downstream conversion, and post-engagement lift are all tied to a real, identified user, not a fingerprint.

Apollo's premise is volume. The consumer equivalent has to be built on consent, because the regulatory and platform layer has closed the door on cold consumer outbound. The good news: the conversion math on consented outbound is dramatically better than scraped outbound ever was.

Who this is for

Consumer-facing brands, D2C, retail, CPG, subscription, services, marketplaces, that have wished for the speed and segmentation of a B2B outbound stack but pointed at the consumer market. mymodel is that layer, with a compliance and performance profile that scraped outbound can't match.
